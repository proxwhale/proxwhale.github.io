Full model

The formula

Firth logistic regression fitted in 89 patients from a single-centre prospective cohort (36 successes). Hydronephrosis enters as a number: grade I = 1, II = 2, III = 3, IV = 4.

logit = 1.3037 − 0.4028 × width (mm) − 0.7938 × hydronephrosis grade + 0.0463 × age (years)
probability of success = 1 / (1 + e−logit)

Points table

One hydronephrosis grade weighs about the same as 2 mm of width, and 10 years of age weigh about the same as −1 mm. This allows a quick mental calculation. Across the range of the sample, it stays within 5 percentage points of the full formula.

Points = width (mm) + 2 × hydronephrosis grade − age / 10

Before use

Limitations

  • Model derived in 89 patients from a single centre, without internal validation by resampling or external validation. It is intended for discussion and research, not for clinical decisions.
  • Range covered by the sample: width 2 to 13 mm, age 20 to 76 years. Grade IV hydronephrosis had only 3 patients; predictions for this grade are unreliable.
  • The age effect is positive (older patients had more success), the opposite of what might be expected. It needs a clinical explanation and confirmation in another sample.
  • Stone density and skin-to-stone distance were not included because they were not associated with success after adjustment for width.
